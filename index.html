About ORTHOS

Where World-Class Orthopaedics Meets Genuine Care

Welcome to ORTHOS — a premier orthopaedic speciality clinic focused on patients with musculoskeletal conditions. Under the leadership of Dr. Prashant Agrawal, our multidisciplinary team of board-certified orthopaedic surgeons, experienced physical therapists, and caring clinical staff works collaboratively to design personalized treatment plans.

Our core objectives are clear: regaining mobility, reducing pain, and improving overall quality of life. Our Specialities

Comprehensive Orthopaedic Care, All Under One Roof

From advanced robotic surgery to non-surgical conservative pain relief — individualized treatments built around your goals.

01

Robotic Joint Replacement

State-of-the-art robotic-assisted knee and hip replacement providing sub-millimeter precision, personalized 3D pre-op planning, and faster recovery.

  • Sub-millimeter Surgical Accuracy
  • Osteoarthritis & Avascular Necrosis (AVN)
  • NABH & JCI Accredited Centre
02

Complex Trauma Care

Comprehensive multidisciplinary management of high-energy open fractures, comminuted breaks, pelvic and acetabular injuries, and polytrauma.

  • Open, Comminuted & Pelvic Fractures
  • Multidisciplinary Trauma Team
  • Limb Salvage & Reconstruction
03

Sports Medicine

Dedicated care for athletes and active individuals — focusing on injury prevention, ligament reconstruction, and accelerated safe return to sports.

  • ACL, PCL & Meniscus Tears
  • Rotator Cuff & Labral Repairs
  • PRP Regenerative Therapy
04

Arthroscopic Shoulder & Knee

Minimally invasive keyhole joint surgery with tiny incisions, minimal tissue disruption, and rapid rehabilitation for shoulder and knee conditions.

  • Rotator Cuff & Shoulder Impingement
  • Recurrent Shoulder Dislocation (Bankart)
  • Cartilage Restoration & Meniscus Repair
05

Comprehensive Fracture Care

End-to-end management for simple, compound, displaced, stress, and avulsion fractures — from gentle closed reduction to advanced internal fixation.

  • Simple, Open & Stress Fractures
  • Cast Immobilization & Plating
  • Bone Healing & Long-Term Follow-up
06

Peri-Prosthetic Joint Care

Specialized tertiary care for complex infections, fractures, and aseptic loosening occurring around previous hip, knee, or shoulder joint replacements.

  • Two-Stage Revision Arthroplasty
  • Peri-Prosthetic Fracture Plating
  • Aseptic Loosening & Pain Evaluation
07

Conservative Management

Non-surgical treatment philosophy: exhaustively utilizing physical therapy, targeted injections, bracing, and lifestyle modification before considering surgery.

  • Osteoarthritis & Tendinitis Care
  • Viscosupplementation & PRP
  • Lifestyle, Ergonomics & Bracing
08

Spine & Back Care

Accurate diagnosis and targeted management for disc herniation, sciatica, and cervical spondylosis with non-surgical decompression and physiotherapy.

  • Slipped Disc & Sciatica Relief
  • Targeted Spinal Injections
  • Postural Rehab & Core Strengthening

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to Common Orthopaedic Questions

Clear, authoritative information directly from Dr. Prashant Agrawal and the ORTHOS clinical team.

ORTHOS is an advanced Orthopaedic Speciality OPD founded and led by Dr. Prashant Agrawal. It is a comprehensive musculoskeletal center providing diagnostic imaging, conservative joint preservation, sports medicine, complex trauma management, and robotic-assisted joint replacement. Operating with a strict Patient-First philosophy, ORTHOS combines cutting-edge clinical technology with compassionate, unhurried care where every patient is treated as a VIP.

The lead specialist at ORTHOS is Dr. Prashant Agrawal (MS Ortho), Senior Consultant Orthopaedic & Joint Replacement Surgeon with over 33 years of orthopaedic experience. He is also a Senior Consultant at Apollo Hospitals Navi Mumbai, specializing in Complex Trauma, Pelvic-Acetabular Fractures, and Robotic Joint Replacement. He is supported by experienced physiotherapists, trauma nurses, and diagnostic specialists.

ORTHOS provides a full spectrum of surgical and non-surgical orthopaedic care, including:
• Robotic Joint Replacements: Total and partial knee and hip arthroplasty with sub-millimeter precision.
• Complex Trauma Care: Open fractures, comminuted fractures, pelvic and acetabular injuries.
• Sports Medicine & Arthroscopy: Keyhole repair for ACL, PCL, meniscus, rotator cuff, and labral tears.
• Peri-Prosthetic Complications: Infection management and revision joint arthroplasty.
• Conservative Orthopaedic Therapy: PRP regenerative injections, viscosupplementation, physiotherapy, and custom bracing.
• Spine Care: Slipped disc and sciatica non-surgical relief.

At ORTHOS, we maintain a strictly transparent and ethical fee policy with no hidden charges. Standard outpatient consultation fees are affordable, competitive, and include thorough clinical assessment and review of previous X-rays/MRIs. For surgical procedures, detailed written estimates covering hospital admission, implant costs, and post-operative follow-up are provided upfront. We also provide complete assistance for cashless mediclaim and health insurance processing through our partner hospital network.

Yes, extensively. ORTHOS specializes in sports medicine for professional athletes, weekend enthusiasts, and fitness individuals. We treat ligament tears (ACL, PCL, MCL, LCL), meniscus damage, shoulder dislocations (Bankart/SLAP lesions), rotator cuff injuries, ankle sprains, and stress fractures. Our clinic combines minimally invasive keyhole arthroscopy with biological therapies (PRP) and guided sports-specific physiotherapy to ensure a safe, full-strength return to play.

Yes. For knees and hips, Dr. Prashant Agrawal utilizes advanced robotic-assisted joint replacement systems at our NABH and JCI accredited center (Apollo Hospitals Navi Mumbai), providing sub-millimeter surgical accuracy and personalized 3D CT surgical modeling. For shoulder conditions (such as rotator cuff tears, labral damage, and impingement), we utilize high-definition minimally invasive arthroscopic (keyhole) surgery, which provides comparable microscopic precision with minimal tissue trauma.

Absolutely. In fact, our primary surgical philosophy is conservative first. Surgery is recommended only when non-surgical therapies cannot provide lasting relief or in acute high-grade trauma. We offer a full range of non-operative treatments including supervised physical therapy, viscosupplementation (hyaluronic acid joint injections), Platelet-Rich Plasma (PRP) regenerative injections, postural correction, lifestyle ergonomics, and specialized orthotic bracing.

Yes, 24/7. Dr. Prashant Agrawal is an authority on complex trauma, open fractures, and polytrauma. Round-the-clock emergency orthopaedic and fracture care is available in coordination with the 24/7 emergency and trauma resuscitation wing at Apollo Hospitals Navi Mumbai. Patients requiring urgent fracture reduction, stabilization, or emergency trauma surgery can call our emergency line +91 91374 00914 for immediate triage and admission assistance.
